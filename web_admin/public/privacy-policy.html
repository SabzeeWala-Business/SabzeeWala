1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to SabzeeWala ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India) and Google Play Developer Policies.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, web dashboard, and related services collect, use, process, and protect your information when you browse our catalog, place orders for fresh vegetables, fruits, and groceries, or utilize our customer wallet.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information essential for fulfilling your orders, providing hyper-local doorstep delivery, processing secure transactions, and preventing fraud.

Data Category Specific Data Collected Primary Purpose
Personal Contact Info Mobile phone number, Name, Optional email address User authentication via OTP, order updates, customer support, and delivery verification.
Delivery Address & Location Street address, landmark, PIN code, and foreground device GPS coordinates (approximate and precise) Identifying nearest distribution outlet, estimating delivery time (10-15 min), and accurate courier navigation to your doorstep.
Payment & Transaction Data Transaction reference IDs, order amount, payment method (UPI / Card / NetBanking / COD). We never store credit card CVV or bank passwords. Processing payments via RBI-authorized payment aggregator (Cashfree Payments) and handling refunds/wallet credits.
Device Information Device model, operating system version, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token Delivering real-time push notifications regarding order progress (Packed, Out for Delivery, Delivered) and silent anti-fraud protection.

3. Location Information Disclosure (Google Play Compliance)

Important Location Notice: SabzeeWala requests access to your device's precise and approximate location (GPS) solely while the application is in the foreground. Location data is used strictly to pinpoint your delivery address, check store serviceability, and guide delivery partners to your exact doorstep. We do NOT track or collect background location when the app is closed or not in use.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Order Processing & Fulfillment: Receiving orders, packaging fresh produce, and scheduling delivery partners.
  • Account Security: Secure passwordless login via MSG91 SendOTP SMS verification.
  • Wallet & Rewards: Managing digital wallet balances, referral coins, and applied promo discount codes.
  • Customer Support: Resolving issues via WhatsApp, phone, or email regarding order refunds or delivery inquiries.
  • Statutory Compliance: Maintaining invoicing records as required by Indian taxation and goods/services laws.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party technology providers to operate our services. These partners are bound by strict confidentiality and security obligations:

  • Cashfree Payments India Pvt. Ltd.: PCI-DSS certified payment aggregator for UPI, debit/credit cards, and net banking processing.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud messaging infrastructure for push notifications and crash reporting.
  • Supabase Inc.: Encrypted cloud database infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • MSG91 (Walkover Technologies): Telecom-compliant transactional SMS OTP gateway for user authentication.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS / TLS 1.3 in transit, encrypted storage at rest). Access to user data is protected by strict role-based access control and Supabase Row Level Security.

We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. Transactional order receipts and tax invoices are retained in accordance with statutory record-keeping regulations (up to 7 years).

7. User Rights & Account Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

You possess full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the SabzeeWala mobile app under Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Portal: You can submit a deletion request online at our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page without having the app installed.
  • Upon processing your request, your profile, delivery addresses, cart, and preferences will be permanently wiped from our active databases within 48 hours.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app and services are directed to general audiences aged 18 and above who can legally enter into commercial contracts. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from children under 13.
