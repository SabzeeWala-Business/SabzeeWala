Delete Your Account & Data

We respect your privacy and give you full authority to delete your personal account and associated data from our servers at any time.

⚠️ What happens when your account is deleted:
  • Your profile details (name, email, mobile number) will be permanently erased.
  • All saved delivery addresses and cart wishlists will be wiped.
  • Unused wallet balances and referral coins will be forfeited.
  • This action is irreversible. You will need to register as a new customer if you return.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the SabzeeWala mobile app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the SabzeeWala app.
  2. Tap on My Profile in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your data will be wiped immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your phone, submit this form. Our team will verify and delete your account within 48 hours.

✅ Thank you. Your deletion request has been registered. You will receive an SMS confirmation once your records are purged.
